2.1.7 Least squares in matrix form

1. Objective

$$S(\beta)=\sum_{i=1}^n (Y_i-X_i'\beta)^2=\varepsilon'\varepsilon=(Y-X\beta)'(Y-X\beta)$$

2. Expand

$$S(\beta)=Y'Y-\beta'X'Y-Y'X\beta+\beta'X'X\beta$$

The two middle terms are $1\times1$ numbers, and each is the transpose of the other, so they're equal:

$$S(\beta)=Y'Y-2\beta'X'Y+\beta'X'X\beta$$

3. Differentiate and set to zero

Rules (the second is the matrix version of $\tfrac{d}{dx}ax^2=2ax$):

$$\frac{\partial(\beta'a)}{\partial\beta}=a\qquad\frac{\partial(\beta'A\beta)}{\partial\beta}=2A\beta\ \ (A\text{ symmetric})$$
$$-2X'Y+2X'X\beta=0\;\Longrightarrow\;\boxed{X'X\hat\beta=X'Y}\;\Longrightarrow\;\hat\beta=(X'X)^{-1}X'Y$$

The inverse exists by ML4. It's a minimum because the second derivative, $2X'X$, is positive definite.

4. Meaning: residuals ⟂ every column of X

$$X'\hat e=0\quad\Rightarrow\quad \sum_i\hat e_i=0\ \text{(ones column)},\qquad \sum_i X_{ij}\hat e_i=0\ \text{(each }X_j)$$

5. Hat matrix

$$H=X(X'X)^{-1}X',\qquad \hat Y=HY,\qquad \hat e=(I-H)Y,\qquad SSR=\hat e'\hat e=Y'(I-H)Y$$

$\hat Y$ is the shadow of $Y$ on the column space of $X$, and $\hat e$ is the perpendicular drop from $Y$ to that shadow. The SSR form needs $H'=H$ and $HH=H$.
