# 05 — Systematic Trader Timeline

> Semester-by-semester milestones from now (Year 3 Sem 2) to graduation and the first role.

---

## Now — Sem 2, Year 3 (Aug–Nov 2026)

**Study:**
- *Quantitative Trading* (Chan) — the most directly applicable book to your current work; read alongside bot development
- *Analysis of Financial Time Series* (Tsay) Ch. 1–4 — ARMA, GARCH, basic time series tools
- Green Book probability puzzles: 3/week
- Mental maths: 10 minutes daily (Optiver-style practice)

**Build:**
- S1 (data pipeline) — clean OHLCV data infrastructure that all future projects depend on
- S2 (tearsheet generator + live monitor) — immediate use for your running bots
- S3 (Kelly sizing implementation) — principled position sizing; extends the QUANTFRAME Kelly note

**Trading:**
- Keep running Gold Pulse and Squeeze Pulse Breakout in live/paper
- Start a trading journal: daily P&L attribution by strategy, observations about when each strategy performed well or poorly

**Milestones by end of semester:**
- [ ] S1 and S2 completed
- [ ] S3 completed: Kelly sizing live for at least one strategy
- [ ] *Quantitative Trading* (Chan) completed
- [ ] Trading journal started with at least 1 entry per week
- [ ] Mental maths: able to do 2-digit multiplication fluently without a calculator

---

## Sem 3 / Year 4 Sem 1 (Jan–Apr 2027, estimated)

**Study:**
- *Algorithmic Trading* (Chan) — strategy-specific chapters (mean reversion, momentum, pairs trading)
- *Advances in Financial ML* (de Prado) — methodology chapters 7–12 (the "is your backtest real" core)
- Tsay Ch. 5–9 — GARCH models and volatility forecasting

**Build:**
- S4 (walk-forward backtest of Gold Pulse) — the rigorous evaluation of your flagship strategy
- S5 (multi-strategy portfolio) — portfolio-level P&L and correlation management
- IMC Prosperity competition (Jan–May) — if announced, register and compete

**Milestones by end of semester:**
- [ ] S4 completed: Gold Pulse walk-forward performance documented with honest analysis
- [ ] S5 completed: three strategies running as a portfolio with correlated Kelly sizing
- [ ] de Prado Part 2 (backtesting) read and applied
- [ ] IMC Prosperity: competed (regardless of placement — the experience matters)
- [ ] Trading journal: 6 months of entries, start looking for patterns in your observations

---

## Final semester / pre-graduation (May–Aug 2027, estimated)

**Study:**
- *Trading and Exchanges* (Harris) — market microstructure
- *Active Portfolio Management* (Grinold & Kahn) Ch. 1–8 — portfolio engineering

**Build:**
- S6 (pairs trading) — broadens beyond trend-following; good interview story
- Polish all existing projects: clean READMEs, unit tests, documented methodology
- Prepare the Gold Pulse walkthrough for interviews (practice out loud — 10 minutes, no notes)

**Job applications:**
- Prop firm graduate/internship programmes: SIG, Optiver, IMC, DRW, Flow Traders — most open applications Sep–Nov for the following summer. Apply in round 1.
- Smaller systematic funds: apply directly; a compelling strategy discussion + track record matters more than brand here
- Have a one-page summary of your trading record (real or paper) ready: Sharpe, max drawdown, Calmar, number of trades, instrument, period

**Milestones before graduation:**
- [ ] S6 (pairs trading) completed
- [ ] At least one prop firm internship application submitted
- [ ] Gold Pulse walkthrough presentation polished
- [ ] 12+ months of trading journal entries reviewed and a summary "lessons learned" written

---

## 1–3 years post-graduation

- Whether or not the first job is a formal prop/HF role: keep running your own strategies in live markets. The track record compounds over time.
- Target: a prop trading desk (as a junior trader) or a pod at a multi-strategy fund within 2 years of graduating
- The ST path has the fastest feedback loop: either your strategies make money or they don't, and the market tells you quickly. Use it.
- Continue building: new strategies, new instruments, new data sources. Alpha decays — the pipeline of ideas is the business.
