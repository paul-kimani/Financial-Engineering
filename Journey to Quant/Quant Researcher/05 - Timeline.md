# 05 — Quant Researcher Timeline

> Semester-by-semester milestones from now (Year 3 Sem 2) to graduation and the first role.

---

## Now — Sem 2, Year 3 (Aug–Nov 2026)

**Study:**
- Blitzstein & Hwang *Introduction to Probability* — full read, all exercises you can manage
- ISLR — cover-to-cover alongside Applied Analytics coursework; the overlap is direct
- *Analysis of Financial Time Series* (Tsay) — Ch. 1–5 (stationary models, GARCH)
- Green Book puzzles: 5 per week

**Research:**
- R1 (factor research notebook) — the foundational project; start with momentum on XAUUSD or a simple equity universe
- R6 (write up one result from coursework as a proper research note) — one per month; start with the Kelly Criterion QUANTFRAME note

**Milestones by end of semester:**
- [ ] Blitzstein & Hwang Chapters 1–8 completed with notes
- [ ] R1 factor notebook complete: signal construction, quintile analysis, IC calculated
- [ ] First R6 research note written and committed to vault
- [ ] 20+ Green Book puzzles worked through (with written solutions, not just answers)
- [ ] Reading log started: one paper read and summarised

---

## Sem 3 / Year 4 Sem 1 (Jan–Apr 2027, estimated)

**Study:**
- *Statistical Inference* (Casella & Berger) — Ch. 1–6: estimation, sufficiency, testing
- *Advances in Financial ML* (de Prado) — methodology chapters (7–12 are the core)
- Shreve I (*Stochastic Calculus for Finance I*) — the binomial model; pairs directly with Financial Calculus coursework
- Continue Green Book + start *Heard on the Street*

**Research:**
- R2 (walk-forward backtest with proper methodology) — the methodological discipline project
- R4 (regime detection study) — builds on existing QUANTFRAME work
- Second R6 research note

**Milestones by end of semester:**
- [ ] R2 completed: CPCV implemented, methodology report written
- [ ] R4 completed: at least two regime-detection methods compared out-of-sample
- [ ] Casella & Berger Ch. 1–6 completed
- [ ] Shreve I through Ch. 4 completed
- [ ] Reading log: 6+ papers summarised
- [ ] One piece of original work polished enough to show in an interview

---

## Final semester / pre-graduation (May–Aug 2027, estimated)

**Study:**
- Shreve II (*Stochastic Calculus for Finance II*) — Brownian motion, Itô calculus, Black-Scholes derivation done properly
- ESL (*Elements of Statistical Learning*) — deepening on the ML theory
- Bishop (*Pattern Recognition and ML*) — Bayesian perspective; relevant for research roles

**Research:**
- R3 (paper replication) — the independent research credibility project
- R5 (volatility forecasting comparison) — advanced; strong MSc application and interview material
- Third R6 research note

**Job/MSc applications:**
- MSc applications: deadline is typically Nov–Jan for UK programmes (Imperial, UCL, Warwick, LSE, Edinburgh)
- Job applications: bank QR internship / graduate programmes, mid-tier hedge fund research programmes
- Build a personal website or GitHub profile with clean write-ups of R1–R5

**Milestones before graduation:**
- [ ] R3 and R5 completed with written research notes
- [ ] MSc applications submitted (if that route)
- [ ] At least one interview obtained and debriefed
- [ ] 4+ research notes in vault, polished enough to share
- [ ] Reading log: 12+ papers

---

## 1–3 years post-graduation (or during MSc)

- Deepen stochastic calculus — Shreve II, then Øksendal
- Start engaging with academic literature independently: SSRN, Journal of Finance, Journal of Financial Economics
- If in an MSc: use the thesis to produce original research that is presentable in interviews
- Target: a top quant HF or research-focused bank role within 2 years of graduating

**MSc recommendation:** If the QR path is the primary target and budget allows, an MSc in Quantitative Finance, Financial Mathematics, or Statistics from a UK/US target programme is the single highest-leverage move you can make. The door it opens at AQR, Two Sigma, Man Group, and similar firms is meaningfully wider. The vault you're building right now is the right preparation for those applications.
