> Recall: The BSM for;
> **Call** : $S_tN(d_1) - Ke^{-r\tau}N(d_2), \quad \tau = t-T$
> **Put:** $Ke^{-r\tau}N(d_2) - S_tN(-d_1)$

Where: 
- $S_t$: Stock price at time $t$.
- $K$: Strike price.
- $\sigma$: Volatility.
- $r$: Risk free rate (constant)
- $\tau = (T-t)$: $T$ is the maturity time.
$$d_1 = \frac{ln \left(\frac{S}{K} \right) + \left( r + \frac{1}{2}\sigma^2\right)\tau}{\sigma \sqrt{\tau}}$$


$$d_2 = \frac{ln\left(\frac{S}{K}\right) + \left( r - \frac{1}{2} \sigma^2\right)\tau}{\sigma \sqrt{\tau}}$$
We also have a standard normal CDF : $N(\cdot)$ or $\Phi (\cdot)$
and the pdf: $N'(x)$ or $\phi (\cdot)$
We differentiate the cdf to get the pdf
also:
$$
\phi(x) = \frac{1}{\sqrt{2\pi}}\cdot e^{-\frac{x^2}{2}}
$$
